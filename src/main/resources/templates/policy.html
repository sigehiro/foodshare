We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share information in the following scenarios:

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience and analyze usage data. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Please check this page regularly for updates.
